Industrial Battery Chargers

Industrial battery chargers (rectifiers) have been designed to provide a very reliable power source for batteries and their connected loads. They can be used with any type of industrial battery. SCR (Thyristor) technology with the transmission angle controlled by a microcontroller (or by analogy circuit on some series) is used. The charger capacity ranges from 50 to 2000 amperes and different voltages  24V,30V, 48V, 110V, 220V…
